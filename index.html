How utilisation rate is calculated
Energy Utilisation Rate (%):
=Total Delivered Energy (kWh)Rated Power (kW) × Available Hours× 100
Example
  • Charger: 1× 180 kW DC Dual-Gun Charger
  • Time Period: 30 days (720 hours)
  • Theoretical Max Energy: 180 kW × 720 hours = 129,600 kWh
  • Actual Metered Energy Discharged (from CSMS/OCPP logs): 12,960 kWh
Energy Utilisation Rate:
=12,960 kWh129,600 kWh× 100 = 10%
